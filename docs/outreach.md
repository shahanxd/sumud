# Outreach drafts

Two messages the founder can send as they are, or edit. Post them under your own name.

## To a prospective Palestinian sensitivity reader

Subject: Reading a game script about Gaza, paid, credited, your notes binding

Assalamu alaikum,

My name is [name] (shahanxd). I am an independent developer in India making SUMUD, a short narrative game about one family on one street in Gaza City over ten days, from a kite festival to a wedding held in a school courtyard. There is no combat and no music with instruments; the score is human voices, and the Quran appears only in held, silent moments with its reference. The game names Gaza and Israel plainly, keeps the camera on the family, and shows the death of a child the way Gaza shows it, as aftermath.

I am not Palestinian, and I will not release this without Palestinian readers. I am looking for two people, ideally from Gaza or with family there, to read the script and the design of each day and tell me what is wrong: dialect, food, customs, names, what a Gazan child would actually say, what we have got wrong without knowing. Your notes will be binding on cultural and linguistic points, you will be credited as you wish (or not at all), and I will pay a modest fee for your time. The script is short, about two hours of play, and I would send it in pieces from mid-November with the full text by early December.

If you are willing, or know someone who might be, I would be grateful for a reply. I am happy to share the design document and the current build first so you can judge whether it deserves your time.

Jazakum Allahu khairan,
[name]

## To a prospective playtester

Subject: Ten minutes of your time to play an unfinished game about Gaza

Hi [name],

I am making a short 2D narrative game about a family in Gaza, built in Godot, and I need people who know nothing about it to play the first ten minutes with no instructions while I watch a screen recording. There are no right answers; I want to see where you get confused and which moment, if any, stays with you.

It takes about fifteen minutes including one question afterwards. You need a Windows or Linux PC and a keyboard or controller. Builds are private and unfinished; the art is silhouettes and the sound is voices only, by design.

If you are up for it, reply and I will send a link and a date. Thank you.

[name]
