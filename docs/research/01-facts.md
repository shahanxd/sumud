# facts

## Summary
Fact-check of 11 claims for SUMUD (2026-09-29). Big corrections: (1) kite FIGHTING with glass string is not a documented Gaza practice in any source opened; it is South Asian/Afghan, and in Aug 2026 Israel declared any kite from Gaza will be treated like a drone, so the kite premise is now literally life-or-death and must be handled with care. (2) The "three-nautical-mile limit" is only true for 2009-2012; the Oslo limit was 20 NM, it swung 3-15 NM over the years, and since Jan/July 2025 the sea has been fully closed with fishermen shot and detained even after the Oct 2025 ceasefire (200 fishers killed by Dec 2024 per UN Protection Cluster). (3) Liyla was not "pulled"; Apple rejected it from the Games category at launch (18 May 2016) and reversed within four days. Dreams on a Pillow raised $240,502 on LaunchGood and now targets a 2027 PC release. (4) October 2026 Next Fest registration closed 31 Aug 2026; the realistic target is February 2027 (22 Feb-1 Mar; register by 10 Jan 2027) and a game may enter only one Next Fest ever. (5) Godot latest stable is 4.7.2 (18 Aug 2026); Arabic/RTL shaping is native via TextServerAdvanced (HarfBuzz/ICU/Graphite); GodotSteam 4.22.1 exists for Godot 4.4+ including 4.7.2, and the project moved from GitHub to Codeberg on 4 Sep 2026. (6) Steam requires disclosure of any AI-generated content that ships in the game or its marketing (policy Jan 2024, clarified 16 Jan 2026); AI coding assistants are exempt; so ElevenLabs voices or Google Flow music would be labelled on the store page. (7) Steam USD prices today: INSIDE $24.99, GRIS $14.99, Valiant Hearts $14.99 (on sale at $3.74). (8) Amiri and Noto Naskh Arabic are both SIL OFL 1.1. (9) Knafeh is Nabulsi; Gaza has a nut-based variant (knafeh ghazawiya); the distinctly Gazan dishes are sumaghiyyeh, rummaniyeh, zibdiyit gambari, dagga, qidra, fukharit adas, with maftoul as a Gazan staple; maqluba and musakhan in the GDD are pan-Palestinian. (10) Haddad is a shared Levantine occupational surname, not Christian-only, but the Hamas Qassam commander Izz al-Din al-Haddad was killed in Gaza City in May 2026, so the family should be renamed; avoid Bakr, Samouni, al-Dalu, Rajab, Doghmush. (11) Latest citable UNICEF cumulative figure: at least 21,289 children reported killed in Gaza between 7 Oct 2023 and 3 Feb 2026 (UNICEF sitrep, 5 Feb 2026); the UN Commission of Inquiry (23 June 2026) puts it at roughly 20,000 killed and 44,000 injured through March 2026; 277 children killed in the first ~9 months of the "ceasefire" (UNICEF, 30 June 2026).

- [verified] 5b. Godot 4 supports Arabic shaping and right-to-left text natively: the default TextServerAdvanced uses HarfBuzz, ICU and SIL Graphite for BiDi, complex layouts and contextual OpenType features; UI is auto-mirrored for RTL locales (anchors/margins, alignment, child order), though the coordinate system and non-UI nodes are not mirrored. The GDD's claim is correct; add the export flag and a font-fallback chain (Amiri + Noto Naskh Arabic) to the tech checklist.
