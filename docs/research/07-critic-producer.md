# critic-producer

## Verdict
Prototype in 7 days: credible, because the pipeline is already real. Godot 4.7.2 is in bin/, the headless smoke test runs, and the beach bot passes 16/16 checks today (carry rules, crawl, kite launch/steer/reel). Ten days of 25-35 minutes each (4-6 hours of hand-built narrative content) by 31 December 2026: not achievable by one part-time AI engineer plus one tester at a quality that survives Steam reviews. That is a 12-18 month game. Budget math: roughly 3 sessions a week of 3-4 hours is 10-12 engineering hours a week, about 150 hours over 13 weeks; the GDD as written needs 250-300. What can honestly ship as content-complete by 31 December is a beta of about 90-120 minutes: ten day cards, four full days (1, 3, 7, 10) of 15-20 minutes and six short days of 5-10 minutes, all played in one street, roof, beach, school courtyard and harbour re-dressed by damage state and light, with 8 named neighbours rather than 20, a vocals-only halal soundtrack from the friend's voice, no voice acting, English-first with a small reviewed Arabic layer. Release should be late January or February 2027, not 31 December, which lands inside the Steam Winter Sale with Valve's reduced holiday review capacity and no press coverage. Expect a 30% slip even on the reduced plan unless session frequency rises; the ordered cut list is in the scope issue. Two things block launch regardless of code: there are no Palestinian readers yet, and the request to use famous reciters' clips and Dr Israr Ahmed lectures cannot ship without licences because those are copyrighted recordings. The founder's other instincts (name 'they', show the perpetrator indirectly, a real explosion, a child's death as reality, more colour) are all right and cheap; 'fight them' must stay defiance in the fiction, never combat mechanics. Price the result at 9.99-12.99 USD, not 17.99.

## Top issues
- [blocker] Scope: ten days at 25-35 minutes cannot be built in 13 weeks; restructure into four tentpole days plus six short days in one shared world
  why: At ~10-12 engineering hours a week (~150 total), a full day with two or three unique physics puzzles, a joy beat, a night scene, a roof scene and founder test cycles costs 15-20 hours; ten of them is 150-200 hours before foundations (~30 h), the menu/options/accessibility/Steam shell (~30 h) and polish (~20 h). Writing volume is also over budget: ten days of dialogue for ~20 neighbours in two languages is more text than two volunteer readers can review in the time available.
  fix: Keep the ten-day structure (day cards, notebook, Teta's question on Day 1 answered on Day 10) but build: tentpoles Day 1 (festival), Day 3 (first strike), Day 7 (evacuation), Day 10 (wedding) at 15-20 min each; Days 2, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9 at 5-10 min with one puzzle each, partly told through notebook pages and camera pans. Build five spaces once (beach, street, home+roof, school courtyard, harbour) with three states (before, siege, after); a day is a lighting state + damage state + who is present. Named neighbours: 8. Ordered cut list, triggered if hours-per-day at the Week 6 gate exceed budget: (1) kite-duel AI -> scripted opponent, (2) Day 6 swimming -> rope/net puzzle on the pier, (3) Days 8 and 9 merged, (4) Days 2 and 5 -> notebook/cinematic days, (5) macOS build -> post-launch, (6) player-kite web campaign -> post-launch. Price 9.99-12.99 USD for ~2 hours.

- [major] Prototype this week must be one 8-12 minute continuous playable, not a feature checklist
  why: The only honest question a prototype answers is whether the carry-and-light loop plus one quiet roof moment make a stranger feel something. Isolated feature demos prove nothing about that; a continuous sequence does, and the beach greybox already provides wind, carry rules and the kite.
  fix: MUST CONTAIN: (a) beach: launch the kite, one kite-carries-an-item puzzle; (b) street: one route puzzle where the jerrycan removes the jump so the route changes; (c) home hub: switch Layla<->Baba, one two-character puzzle (Baba lifts the beam, Layla crawls through); (d) roof at dusk: a sit spot, one Teta line, the friend's a cappella or a code-built vowel-pad placeholder; (e) first strike: birds leave, one real bang, white-out, one generous timed take-cover prompt; (f) street at night: lit vs dark windows, candle traversal; (g) a notebook entry and an end card in tatreez; one hero shot of the beach at dusk at style-bible quality; keyboard plus one controller; bot plays it end to end; founder records one stranger playing it with no instructions. MUST NOT ATTEMPT: a second day, menus or options, saves, Arabic beyond the title, kite-duel AI, procedural IK, adaptive audio, Quran audio (licence unsettled), voices, 20 neighbours, Steam integration, mobile.

- [major] 31 December is the worst Steam release date of the year; be content-complete then and launch late January or February 2027
  why: The Winter Sale (roughly Dec 18-Jan 2) buries new releases; Valve's holiday period reduces store-page and build review capacity (check the Steamworks holiday notice); press is off. Steam also requires the store page to be public for a minimum period before release, build review takes days, and Steam Direct onboarding (identity, bank, tax interview; PAN and W-8BEN for an Indian individual) can take one to three weeks. The October 2026 Next Fest is too soon to register for; February 2027 is the natural demo slot.
  fix: Founder starts Steamworks onboarding in Week 1. Store page in 'Coming Soon' by Week 12. Treat 31 December as content-complete beta, use January for readers' notes and beta fixes, put a Day 1 demo in the February 2027 Next Fest (verify dates on Steamworks), launch 2-4 weeks after. Tell the founder this now, not in December.

- [blocker] 'We go straight in and fight them' must not become combat mechanics
  why: Combat means enemy AI, animation, balance and a second game; it kills pillar 1, invites the propaganda-game dismissal, and adds platform risk for the later mobile release. But 'faceless' was over-corrected; the founder is right that erasing the perpetrator reads as cowardice.
  fix: Name them indirectly and consistently: characters say 'they' and 'them'. The occupation is shown as things, not characters: a persistent drone hum in the ambience, aircraft silhouettes crossing the moon, a tank silhouette at the end of the street on Day 7, the three-mile limit as a line of lights at sea, leaflets falling before the evacuation. 'Fighting' is defiance you play: Baba sails past the limit (Day 6), Karim's confrontation as a playable cinematic with a consequence (Day 8 choice), Teta holding the key at the checkpoint (Day 7). Soldiers appear once, as silhouettes behind lights, never as targets. Confirm this reading with the founder in Week 1 and rewrite pillar 2 to say it.

- [blocker] Famous reciters' clips and Dr Israr Ahmed lectures are copyrighted recordings and cannot ship without licences
  why: The Quran text is free; a specific reciter's recording belongs to the reciter or publisher, and lecture audio belongs to the speaker's foundation. Viral reels are owned three times over. A DMCA takedown on a game about Gaza would be a catastrophic story, and Steam asks about third-party IP at submission.
  fix: Verses and hadith as on-screen text (Arabic calligraphy plus translation, with surah:ayah or collection reference) are always safe. For audio, the friend records the few verses used, or the founder writes to a reciter or foundation for written permission (allow 4-8 weeks and keep the game shippable without it). No lecture clips at launch. Keep docs/rights.md listing every audio file, its source and licence.

- [major] Do not ship Google Flow music or ElevenLabs voices
  why: Flow produces instrumental music, which the halal rule excludes anyway. An AI voice singing a Palestinian grandmother's lullaby in a game about Gaza is the easiest thing for press and community to turn against the project, Steam requires AI-content disclosure on the store page, and ElevenLabs' Gazan dialect quality is unverified.
  fix: The friend sings: quiet room, phone is fine, two takes, plus held vowels on a few pitches; the engineer denoises, layers and builds vocal pads from those vowels in code. TTS only as scratch placeholders in internal builds, flagged and replaced. Write the store-page AI disclosure honestly: art generated by our own open scripts, no AI voices or music.

- [blocker] No Palestinian sensitivity readers; this is a launch gate, not a nice-to-have
  why: An Indian founder and an AI writing Gazan dialogue about a live catastrophe without Palestinian review will be called out and will deserve it; the readers are also the only defence when a detail is wrong.
  fix: Founder starts outreach in Week 1: Palestinian game-dev and Arabic-localisation communities, university Arabic departments, diaspora organisations; offer credit and a modest fee. Target two readers signed by Week 6, script delivered Week 10, notes applied Weeks 12-13. If none by Week 8, the launch date moves; the game does not ship without them. Reduce their load: 8 named neighbours, and Arabic at launch limited to names, songs, verses and key lines.

- [major] The audio section is dead as written; rebuild it as a vocals-only score and make that the game's identity
  why: The GDD's oud, qanun, ney, riq and darbuka score contradicts the founder's halal rule, and a game with no score feels dead, so the replacement has to be designed rather than subtracted.
  fix: Score = human voice + world. The friend's voice is the whole music: a cappella nasheed lines, humming, held-vowel pads layered by code; hand-claps and vocal percussion carry the dabke. Ask the founder explicitly whether the duff (frame drum) is permitted at the wedding; many scholars permit it for weddings, but it is his call. Use the adhan as the game's clock: the day's phases align with prayer times and the call marks each phase change (friend-recorded or permissively licensed). Ambience carries emotion: wind, sea, drone hum, ovens, children. Position it publicly as 'a score made only of voices', the way Limbo's monochrome is an identity.

- [major] Quran audio must never play where the player can fail, pause mid-verse, or loop
  why: Games have been pulled or condemned for recitation over gameplay, failure states or loops; here a checkpoint white-out cutting a verse would offend exactly the audience the game is for.
  fix: Recitation and hadith only in non-interactive, unfailable moments: sit spots, day-end cards, the ending; never during traversal or strikes. Skipping only at phrase boundaries; pause is mute-and-hold, not scrub; text always carries its reference. Founder reviews every placement before beta.

- [major] Procedural IK animation is the riskiest technical line in the GDD
  why: Inside's carry-weight animation was hand-authored by expert animators; code-only IK tends to look floaty, and silhouettes hide texture but not bad motion. It can eat three weeks with nothing to show.
  fix: Pose-tween animation: 8-10 code-generated poses per character (idle, two walk frames, jump, land, crawl, carry-light, carry-heavy, climb, sit), tweened, with lean and speed driven by the carry rules already in game/scripts/player.gd. IK only for feet-on-slope, and only in Week 11 if there is time. Judge it at the Week 3 gate; the fallback is a simple two-bone leg cycle.

- [minor] Timed prompts contradict the accessibility promise; allow three, with an auto-complete toggle
  why: The GDD lists 'no timed button sequences' as a launch requirement and the founder now wants timed surprises; both can be true if the prompts are few and optional.
  fix: Three moments only: Day 3 take-cover, Day 6 the rope at the limit, Day 7 the one thing you grab before leaving. Windows of two seconds or more, a hold alternative, and an options toggle that auto-completes. Get surprise mainly from mechanic removal (Day 7 takes the kite, Day 3 takes the light) rather than QTEs; cinematics come free from the kite-camera scouting already built.

- [major] Showing a child's death changes the rating, the pillar text, and which death it is
  why: 'Children are protected in the fiction' and 'one off-screen death' now conflict with 'death of children can be shown'; left unreconciled, three days will each write it differently.
  fix: The one death is Sami. Show it as aftermath, which is the documented reality: the small shape wrapped in white carried through the street, the name on the wall, his kite. Never the impact, never a body on screen. Layla's own danger stays non-graphic. Retarget to PEGI 16 / possible ESRB M and write the store-page content warning now. Sami's kite is the one Layla flies on Day 10.

- [major] 'Charity: yes, 100%' is ambiguous and has legal and cash consequences
  why: If it means 100% of revenue, the founder pays Steam's cut, Indian tax on foreign remittance and the recoupable fee from nothing, and a public 100% promise is a solicitation claim; Steam does not pay charities directly.
  fix: Founder decides: emphatic yes, or 100% of net. Recommend a fixed public percentage of net after Steam's cut and taxes, a named charity, and a public quarterly ledger. Consult an Indian CA in Week 2 about receiving Valve payouts (PAN, tax interview / W-8BEN, GST).

- [minor] Explosion sound: use a real one, once per strike day, and make it ugly
  why: The founder is right that rumble-only was over-softened, but a hero movie boom is equally wrong.
  fix: The Day 3 first strike and the Day 7 strike each get a real, close, sub-heavy impact (CC0 or recorded), then silence, a tinnitus ring and dust via audio-bus ducking. Everything else is distant thuds in the ambience bed. Keep the rumble bus and the photosensitivity toggle; the loud strike gets its own slider.

- [minor] More colour: yes, as a budget the days spend, not a palette change
  why: Pure black silhouettes over two hours turn monotonous; Limbo is oppressive by design and Inside added muted colour. But colour everywhere destroys the read of kites as the only pure colour.
  fix: Three tiers: kites (full saturation, always); warm light spill (bread, candles, tatreez red, the wedding) that grows with joy beats; sky and sea (dawn gold, dusk rose, siege grey). One per-day colour-budget value drives every shader: joy beats raise it, siege drains it, Day 10 spends everything. Cheap because the look is already palette-driven, and it makes the Day 10 sky pay off.

- [minor] The player-kite web campaign is a moderated web service; move it to post-launch
  why: It needs a site, a moderation queue (people will submit hateful names and patterns), a pipeline into builds and privacy handling; that is a month of work and a liability at launch.
  fix: Launch with an in-game 'design a kite' screen whose kites appear in your own Day 10 sky, plus a curated batch from Discord and testers baked in. Ship the public web version after launch.

- [major] The founder is the only tester
  why: Every gate in the plan depends on a stranger's reaction; the founder is not a stranger and the engineer cannot play.
  fix: Recruit 5 testers by Week 4 (friends of friends, Discord, itch private builds) and 8 by Week 11. Protocol: screen-record, no help, note the first confusion, ask one question after: 'what moment stuck?'. The founder owns this list.

- [major] Code-generated silhouettes can read as a tech demo; lock the look at Week 3 with a hero-shot test
  why: Noise-roughened polygons plus paper grain can be beautiful or look like an asset flip in black; the difference is composition and light, which need iteration with eyes on it.
  fix: Week 3 gate: three hero shots shown to three strangers with no context; if two do not call it beautiful or striking, change approach (hand-touched SVG silhouettes or sourced CC0 silhouettes the founder can pick). Use the shot_driver diff suite already in game/tests for nightly regression.

- [major] Arabic without a native speaker: keep it small and technically verified
  why: Godot shapes RTL through ICU, but bidi in mixed Arabic/English subtitle lines, font fallback and diacritics on calligraphy cards need real testing, and dialect errors are a credibility risk.
  fix: Week 2: an RTL test scene with Amiri and Noto Naskh Arabic, mixed-direction lines, captured in the screenshot diff suite. Launch Arabic = names, songs, verses, key lines and menus; full dialogue Arabic after the readers' review.

- [minor] Session hygiene: one uncommitted change is sitting in the tree
  why: D:\Downloads\mech\sumud\game\scripts\camera_rig.gd is modified and uncommitted, against the rule that every session ends with a running, committed build.
  fix: Commit or revert it at the start of the next session; add a script (tools/check.ps1) that runs the headless smoke test and bot before every commit.

## Missed opportunities
- Steam Deck as a first-class target: Godot plus full controller support plus 16:9 makes Deck Verified nearly free, and the Deck audience buys narrative indies; ship Windows and Linux at launch and drop macOS until a Mac tester exists.
- The adhan as the game's clock: the day's phases already map onto prayer times, the call is unambiguously halal and free to record, and it gives the mosque a presence without a single line of dialogue.
- The duff (frame drum) at the wedding, if the founder permits it: many scholars allow it for weddings, and it gives Day 10 a heartbeat without breaking the rule.
- Urdu and Hindi localisation the founder can review himself: the South Asian solidarity audience is enormous, and it is the one language pass that needs no outside reader; move Urdu from the post-launch list to launch plus one month.
- The Kite Runner's literal act, kite running (chasing the fallen kite through the street), is a ready-made traversal device for Day 1 and Day 9; its themes of loyalty and guilt belong quietly to Karim and Sami, not to the plot.
- Open-source the tools/ generators as the answer to Steam's AI disclosure and as the devlog engine: 'the street grows itself' GIFs from the existing shot_driver are free marketing.
- The headless bot and screenshot diff already exist; an hour of GitHub Actions with the console binary turns them into a nightly play-every-day CI that catches a broken puzzle the same night.
- A Day 1 demo for the February 2027 Next Fest if the launch moves, which it should; Next Fest is once per game, so do not waste it on October.
- The notebook ('nothing is judged, everything is remembered') is the store-page and trailer hook; show the notebook filling in, not just silhouettes.
- Post-strike silence: audio-bus ducking and a tinnitus tone are cheap and are the actual emotional payoff of using a real explosion.
- Mobile later costs nothing now if the 8-input rule, touch-sized UI and 16:9 safe areas are kept; note Apple's 2016 precedent with Liyla means Android first when the time comes.
- A sources tab per day doubles as the press defence and the answer to 'why is an Indian founder making this'.

## Keep
- The pipeline: Godot 4.7.2 in bin/, headless smoke test green, beach bot 16/16, assets as code, every session ends in a commit. Most indies never have this; it is the project's strongest asset and the reason a 7-day prototype is credible.
- Carry as the spine of every system, and light in windows as the only status display with no morale bar and no quest log.
- Shadow and Thread with tatreez interface and kites as the colour carriers, extended with a per-day colour budget rather than replaced.
- Tentpole days 1, 3, 7, 10 and the vertical-slice gate (Days 1 plus 3) before any other day is built; if the slice fails, nothing else would have worked either.
- The eight-input control rule, full remapping, and no voice acting at launch, which also sidesteps the AI-voice trap entirely.
- Specific not generic: Gaza named, real food, real places, fictional people, true situations, a sources tab, no slogans, and readers whose notes are binding.
- The ending as written: the wedding happens, they are still there, and one kite rises per remembered act of care, now with Sami's kite in Layla's hands.
- The founder's instincts: 'they' in dialogue, the perpetrator shown indirectly, a real explosion, a child's death as reality, more colour, absolutely halal audio; all accepted with the constraints in the issues.
- Repository layout and README setup steps, which are accurate (the 4.7.2 binary path and headless command work as written).
- The notebook as journal, save summary and memory of choices; choices that leave marks, not branches.
