# critic_v2_63e13029d8f

## Verdict
The core is real but not yet a game. Carry, Kite, Light and Street are four systems of care with no counter-pressure, no numbers, and no character to carry the grief the founder now wants shown. The document is production-ready in tech and pre-production in design, which is backwards for a prototype due this week. Three decisions unlock everything. First, make the drone the "they": a sound and a shadow with a 0-1 intensity that closes and opens the sky, so every kite flight and every lit window is a small defiance and the kite has a reason to exist on siege days. Second, make Sami and a Day 1 promise the spine: a message line strung between two roofs on Day 5, his death on Day 6 night seen through the kite's own camera, his broken kite as a carry choice on Day 7, and Days 9-10 as the promise kept. That integrates "show children's deaths" and The Kite Runner's tone without its plot, and it is not a twist because the player has already learned what a kite in the sky means. Third, rebuild the audio as one human voice, the daf at the wedding, world sound, and specific ayat at the specific moments a Gazan family would say them; the maqam/oud plan breaks the halal rule outright, and AI Quran or AI Arabic voices would end the game's community standing on release day. Cut this week to a five-minute kite proof, cut a day to 12-15 minutes so ten days fit inside the founder's window, and this can be loved.

## Top issues
- [blocker] The production plan does not fit the founder's dates: a 40-minute two-day slice is not a working prototype this week, and ten days at 25-35 minutes (about five hours) of narrative game with generated art, music, tests and a slice gate cannot land by end of December with one engineer.
  why: Playdead spent six years on Inside and Nomada three on Gris; even with AI-assisted code the bottleneck is level design and playtest iteration, not typing. If scope stays, the pillars that this subject cannot survive without (joy every day, binding readers, accessibility) get cut under pressure.
  fix: This week = a five-minute proof, not a slice: one beach scene, a real wind field (64x36 vector grid, Perlin drift plus gusts, dust advected by it), Layla run/jump/one ledge, kite launch/steer/reel, a scripted Sami kite and the duel cut, the cut kite chased by kids, one two-handed carry (knafeh tray) home through two screens with one set-down obstacle, one roof sit with the singer's hum and Teta's question, a far light on the horizon, tatreez title. Pass/fail: a stranger smiles at the duel. Then redefine a day as 12-15 minutes (ten days, about 2.5 hours, Inside/Gris length at the planned price) and treat end of December as Days 1-3 polished for Next Fest with every system present, full game after. Put both options in writing to the founder now.

- [blocker] The entire audio section (oud, qanun, ney, riq, darbuka, piano, strings, maqam MIDI through soundfonts, synth pads) is disallowed by the founder's no-haram-instruments rule, and each suggested substitute (famous reciter clips, Dr Israr Ahmed lectures, Google generative music, ElevenLabs) is copyrighted, tonally wrong for a Gazan family, or reputationally fatal.
  why: Recitation recordings and lectures are copyrighted and Content ID will claim every streamer's video, which kills the doc's own streamer strategy. An Urdu lecture inside a Gaza City home breaks 'specific, not generic'. AI-generated Quran will contain tajweed errors and will be received as desecration. AI Arabic dialogue in place of Palestinian voices is the exact cowardice the doc warns against. Generative music tools output instruments.
  fix: Score = one human voice (the singer friend, multitracked: humming for calm, a mawwal line for grief, zaghrouta for joy, children's a cappella in the stairwell school), daf only at the wedding (widely cited hadith permit the daf at weddings; get the founder's scholar to confirm in writing), and world sound as the harmonic bed (sea, wind, oven, cats, adhan). Palestinian women's wedding songs and men's sahja are traditionally a cappella with clapping, so this is more authentic, not less. Quran at events, recorded by a local qari, subtitled with a public-domain English translation (Pickthall): 2:155-157 the first time a name goes on the wall; 2:153 at the first stairwell lesson; 16:14 as Baba pushes off on Day 6; 3:200 on Day 7 when the family decides to walk; 94:5-6 when the lights return on Day 8; 30:21 at the nikah on Day 10. One hadith, Day 8, for Karim: the strong one controls himself in anger. No lectures, no sermons: verses are said by the family at the moment a Gazan family would say them. Explosions: the first strike (end of Day 2) is the one full-volume real explosion in the game, then six seconds of tinnitus and the title card; every later strike is rumble, light and silence because the family stops hearing them as events; Sami's is pure silence. Say no to Google generative music and ElevenLabs in one sentence each.

- [blocker] The emotional spine is missing: the 'one off-screen death' has no name, Sami exists only on Day 1, and the founder's 'show children's deaths', The Kite Runner reference and Day 9's 'street rebuilds a kite' are all dangling with no character to hang on.
  why: Without a specific loss the ending sky is sentiment without cost; with an arbitrary loss it is a twist, which the doc rightly forbids. The founder's asks converge on one design.
  fix: Sami is the co-protagonist without being playable. Day 1 promise after the duel, made whoever wins: 'Whoever wins flies at the wedding. Whoever loses builds the kite.' Both outcomes of a real duel produce a promise, so the duel result is a genuine choice made with the hands. Sami has a beat every day 1-6: football Day 2, holds the candle in the bakery Day 3, jerrycan rival Day 4, the message line strung between the two roofs on Day 5 (the friendship across a kite line, literally), watching for Baba's boat Day 6. Day 6 night: Sami flies alone on the far roof; the player learned on Day 5 that a kite is visible from above; the drone hum rises; Layla's kite camera is on him; white; his kite, the only coloured thing on screen, falls into frame and settles at Layla's feet. Shown, no gore, no twist, caused by no player choice, never a fail state or punishment. Day 7: his broken kite is a one-hand item in the carry choice. Day 9: the street rebuilds it with one part from each family verb (thread from the dress tatreez, paper from Mama's books, spars from Baba's boat, paste from Abu Ahmad's flour, string from Karim's wire). Day 10: Layla keeps the promise. Borrow from The Kite Runner only: the tournament as childhood's last day, kite running as devotion, a promise as the ending's engine, a father whose approval matters (Baba and Karim). Do not borrow: betrayal, guilt, class asymmetry between the friends, a return-and-rescue plot, adult narration, or any echo of 'a thousand times over'.

- [blocker] Carry, Kite, Light and the strike sequence are described as feelings, not rules: no speed multipliers, no bag rules, no kite control model, no wind representation, no physical way to allocate light, no warning timings, no definition of what turns a day phase.
  why: An engineer cannot start and a designer cannot tune what is not stated. 'Who gets light' as written is a management menu in disguise, contradicting the doc's own no-menus stance on carry.
  fix: Carry: bag = 3 slots for small items (matches, key, phone, bread, message, notebook); one-hand heavy (half jerrycan, battery, flour sack, radio, toolbox) = 80% speed, low jump, slow climb, no crawl; two-hand (full jerrycan, drum, beam, child, wounded adult) = 60% speed, no jump, no climb, cannot open doors, one press to set down. Layla cannot lift two-hand adult loads; Baba can; Teta carries bag only; Karim carries one-hand heavy at 100%. Kite: hold Kite to launch when local wind exceeds a threshold shown by a dust gust at Layla's feet; left stick moves Layla and string pays out from the kite's pull; right stick tilts the kite plus or minus 30 degrees to move across the wind; trigger reels in; max string 30 m; camera frames the midpoint and zooms with distance; obstacle contact tumbles the kite; a snagged kite is retrievable by climbing on Days 1-2 and lost on siege days. Duel: strings are segments; at a crossing the string with higher relative tangential speed cuts; a 0.5 s pull window (hold and release) on contact is the game's first timed press. Light: lamps, candles and batteries are carry objects; every home has a lamp hook by the window; a lit lamp on a hook lights the window; one fuel = one night; the phone light is personal, infinite and dim (2 m) so no player is ever soft-locked; candle medium and consumable; lamp large and consumable, and some interactables can only be read in lamp light. Strike: T0 hum to 1.0 and birds leave, T+2 s rumble, T+4 s flash; cover = any interior cell or doorway marker; failure = white, checkpoint at last doorway. Phases turn on four flags: bag chosen and house left; the day's puzzle solved; at least one lamp placed; sat on the roof. Checkpoint at every doorway and every two-hand set-down. Non-controlled family members return to the home hub, never stranded.

- [blocker] Nothing pushes back moment to moment: all four systems are acts of care, the kite is absent on Days 3, 4, 6, 7 and 8, and the founder's 'they' has nowhere to live in the mechanics.
  why: Inside works because every screen has a pressure; a care-only loop reads as chores. The signature verb vanishing exactly when the game turns serious is the worst place to lose it.
  fix: The drone is 'they'. An audio loop with a 0-1 intensity on a per-day schedule and a shadow that crosses the ground. Above 0.6 the sky is closed: Layla refuses to launch (one line), neighbours dim their own windows (the community display responds), outdoor NPCs go in. Below it, the sky opens. The player reads the sky like a tide and plans around it. This makes every flight a defiance, gives Day 5's message line and Day 6's night their dread with no timer, and puts the kite on every day: Day 3 scouting the collapsed bakery from above, Day 4 spotting which end of the street the water truck stopped at, Day 6 signalling the boat from the beach, Day 7 reading the route over the rubble to the school, Day 8 lifting the generator wire to the roof. Add three more indirect faces: the gunboat searchlight and distorted loudhailer at the three-mile line on Day 6, the leaflets on Day 7 (a map and an arrow, no flag), and the 1948 key, which already names everything for anyone who knows history. Characters say 'they' freely. Amend pillar 2 to 'no face, but a presence'.

- [major] The title card lands around minute 60, after two full 25-35 minute days of wedding shopping, inside Steam's refund window and past most streamers' first-impression window.
  why: The doc's argument (fall in love before the threat) is right; the dose is wrong. The Kite Runner's structure is the tournament immediately followed by the fall, and Day 1 already is the tournament.
  fix: Day 1 = 15 min: festival, duel, kite running, the promise, knafeh, the carry home, the roof question, a far light on the horizon that nobody comments on. Day 2 = 12 min: wedding shopping under a new unease, the drone's first hum, playable football at sunset, Teta's stories. Day 2 night: the strike on the street, the one real explosion, six seconds of tinnitus, title card at about minute 27. Days 3-7 stay as siege; the act count stays three.

- [major] Twenty named neighbours with daily changing needs and unannounced callbacks is 200 need-states plus a payoff matrix, with no mechanism for how a player discovers a need without a quest log.
  why: This is the most lovable feature in the doc and the most likely to ship as generic NPCs with one bark each.
  fix: Slice = six neighbours, one arc each: Abu Ahmad (bread), Um Samir (clinic light), Um Sami (Sami's mother), Abu Khalil the old fisherman (cards), the twins (jerrycan racers), Hajja Fatima (a charger). Needs are world states, never text: door open = come in; window dark = needs light; chalk marks on the wall = race scores; a plate left outside = they cooked for you. A callback CSV with three columns (act id, day done, payoff day/scene/line): water to Hajja Fatima on D4 pays off on D10 as the seat beside her saved for Layla; bread to the twins on D3 pays off on D9 as the paper for Sami's kite. Full game caps at 14 neighbours and one run can help about 60% of them, so the empty chairs at the wedding are the reason to play again.

- [major] Days 8-10 sit in one courtyard, Day 9's mechanic focus is literally 'everything together', and the act that carries the game's thesis has no stake beyond a generator.
  why: Three days in one location running recap mechanics is where players stop, and it is the act that has to earn the ending.
  fix: Day 8 stake = Karim's choice reframed under the founder's 'fight them' note: leave on the boat, or stay and dig. Dig is a real verb: Karim and the young men dig out the bakery with two-hand loads while the drone closes the sky over them. Day 8's joy beat = Karim fixes the courtyard speaker and the adhan sounds from the school for the first time since the mosque was hit (halal, precise, huge). Day 9 = rebuilding Sami's kite as a five-part puzzle across the courtyard plus a cappella dabke with daf and the dress. Day 10 = the nikah (30:21), the walk to the beach, the flight. Advise against an armed-resistance third option for Karim: it moves the rating to 18/M, invites platform review, and destroys pillar 2's 'impossible to dismiss as a pamphlet'. Baba not turning the boat back under the searchlight, Karim's shovel, and Layla flying when the sky says no are the game's own language for 'not cowards'; the founder should choose knowingly.

- [major] Most joy beats are cutscenes the player watches (bread by candlelight, kids racing, the catch), and one (Teta refusing to leave the key) is a character beat, not joy.
  why: Joy that is played is remembered; joy that is shown is skipped. Pillar 3's playtest rule needs a delivery rule or it will be met with cutscenes.
  fix: At least half of each day's joy must be player-initiated and ungated: kite duels available any day the sky is open; football on the beach playable with Karim's run/jump/kick against two kids; the jerrycan race is a playable footrace against the twins; cards with Abu Khalil is a sit spot; cats follow whoever carries fish. Day 7's joy = the kids singing on the walk and the cat coming along; Teta and the key becomes a systemic gag (see missed opportunities). The zaghrouta is the game's joy stinger, recorded once from the singer friend, used everywhere.

- [major] The founder asks for more colour and the doc's answer (kites only) is purer than it is right; Limbo-grey silhouettes for the whole game will read as one note.
  why: Inside and Badland are both silhouette games with strongly coloured light and skies; the premium feel comes from coloured light on black shapes, not from grey.
  fix: Honest answer: yes, more colour, in three places and never on the silhouettes. The sky and sea (dawn gold, day cerulean, dusk rose, siege ochre-grey, night indigo); interior lamplight; food and cloth (knafeh orange, maqluba, the red tatreez of the dress, hung fabric in the courtyard). Colour budget follows the story: Day 1 is the most saturated screen in the game so the first screenshot anyone takes is joy; Days 3-7 drain; Days 8-10 bring colour back object by object as the street recovers, ending in the sky of kites. Kites remain the only saturated moving objects, so they still sing.

- [major] The founder wants surprise (timed presses) and cinematics; the doc promises 'no timed button sequences' as an accessibility line and defines no cinematics at all.
  why: Both can hold only if timed moments are few, diegetic and bypassable, and cinematics never take the controls away.
  fix: Exactly five timed moments, all diegetic: the duel cut (0.5 s pull), strike cover (4 s), the net under the searchlight on Day 6 (hold to keep hauling while the light is on you; release and the catch slips), the generator pull-cord rhythm on Day 8, and the one visible clock in the game: Day 7's evacuation order says one hour, which is eight real minutes to load up and get everyone out, a game that never had a timer suddenly has one. Accessibility menu: extend or disable each. Cinematics: five, letterboxed, controls retained in the Playdead manner: the opening sea and kite; the title card (the only three seconds without control); Sami's kite falling; the evacuation walk as a long tracking shot while the player still walks; the final sky.

- [major] Four named choices and 'everything is remembered' is not a reason to play twice, and the doc gives players nothing of their own to show.
  why: The games that get loved give players an artefact to share (Unpacking's rooms, Spiritfarer's hugs); the doc's community plan depends on that and does not provide it.
  fix: The duel outcome changes the promise's shape. The wedding photo at the end is composed from the callback table and exports as an image. The notebook exports per day. A run helps about 60% of the street. Day select after completion carries your notebook. Kite duels and football unlock as menu mini-games, with a local two-player duel mode (the duel is already two-player). The title screen after completion shows your Day 10 sky with your dedicated kites.

- [minor] Showing a child's death, even in silhouette, likely moves the target from PEGI 12 / ESRB T to PEGI 16 or T with descriptors; the rating line and press angle assume otherwise.
  why: Valiant Hearts is PEGI 12 with off-screen deaths; This War of Mine is 18/M. This design sits between and the store page must not promise the lower one.
  fix: Decide the rating target before the Steam page. Keep no blood, no death animation, no fail-state deaths and no player-caused deaths, and 16/T stays defensible.

- [minor] There are no Palestinian readers, the founder is Indian, and the doc makes reader notes binding; a public build without them is the single largest reputational risk in the project.
  why: The community will read the game's authorship before its craft, and the Day 6 scene in particular cannot ship on the founder's judgement alone.
  fix: Before any public build, approach the creator of Liyla and the Shadows of War (Rasheed Abueideh) and Palestinian developer and community collectives; budget for two readers even though the singer is free; credit readers by name if they agree; make the dialect pass a roadmap gate, not a note.

- [minor] 'Charity: yes, 100%' is ambiguous; if it means all revenue there is no money for readers, the Steam fee, a porting partner or studio time for the singer.
  why: It is on the store page and audited, so it has to be exact before the page goes live.
  fix: Ask the founder for a percentage of net after platform fees and reader costs, state it as written, audit yearly as the doc already says.

## Missed opportunities
- The 1948 key as a systemic gag: it can be tried in every door in the game; nothing opens; Teta has a different line each time; on Day 10 she tries it on the school gate. The game's thesis as a running joke players will clip.
- Food as play: cook one dish per act with what the street actually has (maqluba Day 2, bread Day 3, fish Day 6, wedding knafeh Day 10); the real recipe goes into the notebook. The Venba effect: players cook it at home and post it. Halal, apolitical, and the most colourful thing in the world.
- The sea every day, not just Day 6: every roof looks at it; gunboat lights on siege nights; the three-mile buoys visible from the roof; the beach as the one wide sky. Pillar 4 says 'one sea' and the design forgets it.
- Gaza's street cats: they follow whoever carries fish; one adopts the family and survives everything. Free lovability and a wordless joy beat on the worst days.
- A tatreez motif catalogue by village with real names, unlocked as chapter cards stitch themselves, browsable in extras. Players learn where each pattern comes from.
- A notebook surprise: on Day 8 a page appears in Sami's handwriting (the note he sent down the line on Day 5). The founder's 'surprise the player' done with a pencil.
- The dedicated-kite gallery in the game itself: on Day 10 the player walks the beach and reads dedications before the flight, so the kite campaign is a place, not a background.
- Day 1 as the free demo with the duel as the shareable ten-second clip; every preview build already opens on it.
- Karim's fixed radio on Day 8 is the one place a famous recitation could live if the founder insists; licence it properly or skip it. Everywhere else the family's own voices are stronger.
- Photo mode at sit spots with a tatreez frame and the day's notebook line burned in, the Unpacking screenshot moment.
