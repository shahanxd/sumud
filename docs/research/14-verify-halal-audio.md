## Overall
The research is largely sound: every fiqh source cited was opened and the headline positions (instruments prohibited except the duff in the strict-mainstream view; Dar al-Ifta as the permissive outlier; 'what reaches the ears' as the test for vocal and synthesised imitation; artillery and mechanical sounds permitted; Quran never as a bed, stop only at verse ends; nasheeds exempt from that rule; islamqa's video-clip conditions; AI voice-cloning needs the voice owner's permission; no auto-tune on Quran) are accurately quoted. Three corrections matter for the studio. (1) Finding 6: the Standing Committee in islamqa 20047 was asked about men clapping with children and classmates, not weddings; the fatwa never mentions weddings, so the 'strict view on wedding clapping' is an extrapolation from Ibn Baz's remark about parties. (2) Finding 12: the permissive Dar al-Ifta AI ruling is real but was issued by Grand Mufti Nazir Ayyad and reported on 3 March 2026 (restated 4 Aug 2026), not 18 January 2026; it permits AI electronic mushafs and synthetic recitations only as aids for correcting recitation and memorisation, subject to prior review by the bodies that check the mushaf. The January 2026 Dar al-Ifta ruling in English media was the ban on using ChatGPT-type AI for Quran interpretation. This actually strengthens the recommendation not to ship AI-generated Quran. The MUI 30/2023 PDF is a scan, so its wording is confirmed only through secondary summaries. (3) Finding 14: MEMO 2016 calls the Beit Lahia mass wedding charity-funded with Haniyeh attending as a guest, not Hamas-organised; the CS Monitor 1998 mass wedding was in Nablus (West Bank), not Gaza; and the Al Jazeera 2026 'largely disappeared' and 'no longer feasible' lines are paraphrases that do not appear in the article, which instead says weddings 'used to be' dabke-and-zaghrouta occasions and that couples now marry in tents, near-Yellow-Line halls or mass weddings. Smaller fixes: Hüller's prize is the Silver Bear for Best Leading Performance; Incredibox is now at 3.3M paid downloads (Oct 2025 press kit); islamweb 86698 calls a meaning-changing stop 'not only a sin, rather an act of disbelief'; the Sept 2024 Al-Azhar and Dar al-Ifta warnings were parallel statements, not one joint declaration; islamweb 351643's 'not scantily clad' is the questioner's wording; Raseef22's 'preparatory and secondary' phrase is a teacher describing custom, HRW names no schools, and Wikipedia's 'preparatory stage' is grades 1 to 4 (PA scheme), so the grades 7 to 9 / age-12 threshold rests on an unverified UNRWA convention. Darul Fiqh's AI-voiceover answer was by a trainee mufti reviewed by Mufti Faraz Adam. All recommendations survive these corrections; the wedding-scene sourcing should drop the CS Monitor citation and soften the Hamas and Al Jazeera 2026 language.

Dar al-Ifta 4866's condition reads 'a woman does not sing in front of non-relative males'. islamqa 222730 does restrict the Eid hadith to 'young girls (meaning that they had not yet reached puberty)' who 'were not known to be singers'. No fatwa on a recorded female voice inside a game was found in this check either, so the 'likely' rating and the mitigation options are reasonable.
