## Verdict
Verified today (29 Sep 2026) on this machine: Godot 4.7.2 sits in bin/, the headless smoke test passes, and the beach bot passes 16/16 (run, jump, carry slows the run, carry removes the jump, crawl under a wall, kite launch/climb/steer/reel). Three commits, about 1,100 lines, a tatreez generator and a self-stitching chapter card exist. That makes the 7-day prototype credible, but only as one continuous 8-12 minute playable, not the GDD's 40-minute Days 1+3 slice. The one screenshot is a greybox (flat bands, rectangles, no grain, no light); the look itself is unproven and the project is still on the Compatibility renderer, which cannot do the glow the art direction depends on.

Ten days of 25-35 minutes by 31 December is not achievable. Budget: sessions of a few hours several times a week is 10-12 engineer hours a week, roughly 140-155 hours over 13 weeks. The GDD as written needs 250-300: a full day with two or three unique physics puzzles, a night scene, a roof scene, dialogue in two languages and founder test cycles costs 15-20 hours; foundations about 30; menu/options/accessibility/Steam shell about 30; polish about 20. Dreams on a Pillow, a Palestinian team of 12 with $240k raised, is taking two-plus years. What can honestly be content-complete by 31 December: a 90-120 minute game with ten day cards, four tentpole days (1, 3, 7, 10) at 15-20 minutes and six short days at 5-10 minutes, played in five spaces (beach, street, home+roof, school courtyard, harbour) re-dressed by damage state and light, 8 named neighbours not 20, a vocals-only score built from the friend's recorded stems, no voice acting, English-first with a small reviewed Arabic layer, Windows and Linux. Quality target: a strong Steam narrative indie, not Inside. Expect a 30% slip even on that plan unless session frequency rises.

31 December is the worst Steam date of the year: it sits inside the Winter Sale (17 Dec 2026 to 4 Jan 2027), Valve's review capacity drops over the holidays, and press is off. October 2026 Next Fest registration closed 31 August; February 2027 (22 Feb to 1 Mar, register by 10 Jan) is the natural demo slot and each game gets exactly one. Treat 31 December as content-complete beta and launch after 1 March 2027, or late January without Next Fest. Price 9.99-12.99 USD for two hours, not 17.99.

Two things block launch regardless of code: there are no Palestinian readers, and the famous reciters' clips and Dr Israr Ahmed lectures are copyrighted recordings. Fact-checks also change content: glass-string kite duels are a South Asian practice with no Gaza source (cut them, which also deletes a system); in August 2026 Israel declared any kite from Gaza is treated as a drone, so the ending is now literal defiance; the 'three-mile limit' was true only 2009-2012 and the sea has been closed since 2025; rename the Haddad family (a Qassam commander of that name was killed in Gaza City in May 2026). The founder's instincts (say 'they', show the perpetrator indirectly, a real explosion, a child's death as reality, more colour, absolutely halal audio) are right and cheap. 'We go straight in and fight them' must stay defiance in the fiction and never become combat mechanics.

  fix: The Day 3 first strike and the Day 7 strike each get a real, close, sub-heavy impact (Sonniss GDC or Kenney CC0, never a ripped livestream), then silence, a tinnitus ring and dust via audio-bus ducking. Everything else is distant thuds and the constant zanana drone in the ambience bed. Keep the rumble bus and the photosensitivity toggle; the loud strike gets its own slider.

- [minor] More colour: yes, as light and atmosphere the days spend, not paint on the silhouettes
  why: Pure black silhouettes over two hours turn monotonous; Limbo is oppressive by design and Inside added muted colour. Every premium reference gets colour from light, sky, haze and one or two accents, or from a painter; flat coded colour reads cheap when it is large, untextured, banded or saturated at mid-brightness.
  fix: Three tiers: kites (full saturation, always); warm light spill (bread, candles, tatreez red, the wedding) that grows with joy beats; sky and sea (dawn gold, dusk rose, siege grey). One per-day colour-budget value in JSON drives every shader: joy beats raise it, Day 3 drains it, Day 10 spends everything. Two hue families plus one accent per scene, never more.

- [minor] The player-kite web campaign is a moderated web service; move it to post-launch
  why: It needs a site, a moderation queue (people will submit hateful names and patterns), a pipeline into builds and privacy handling; that is a month of work and a liability at launch.
  fix: Launch with an in-game 'design a kite' screen whose kites appear in your own Day 10 sky, plus a curated batch from Discord and testers baked in. Define the kite JSON format now so the public web version can ship after launch without touching the game.

- [minor] Session hygiene: three .uid files are uncommitted and docs/ and tests/ are empty
  why: game/scripts/chapter_card.gd.uid, game/tests/shot_card_driver.gd.uid and game/tests/shot_driver.gd.uid are untracked; Godot 4.4+ generates .uid files and expects them committed so references survive renames. docs/ and tests/ are empty directories the README promises content for, and the GDD lives only in the scratchpad.
  fix: Commit the .uid files at the start of the next session; copy the GDD and this plan into docs/; add tools/check.ps1 that runs the headless smoke test and bot before every commit; an hour of GitHub Actions with the console binary turns the bot and screenshot diff into a nightly play-every-day CI.
