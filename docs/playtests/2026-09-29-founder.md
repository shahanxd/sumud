# Playtest 1: the founder plays the first playable (29 September 2026)

The first time anyone other than the test bots played SUMUD. It found four bugs the bots had missed, and it produced the verdict that moved the project from "build more days" to "lock pre-production first". This file is the record; `docs/production/pipeline.md` is what we do about it.

| | |

| Machine | ASUS VivoBook, Windows 11, Intel Iris Xe Graphics (Vulkan 1.3.215, Forward+ renderer), 1920 by 1080 screen, game in a maximised window of 1920 by 1010 |

## What happened, in order

1. **The game would not start properly.** Running `bin\Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64.exe --path game` on a fresh clone printed `Could not find type "Beat"` and font-loader errors, and showed only a yellow sky over a blue sea. Cause: a fresh checkout has no `.godot/` import cache, so the engine had not yet indexed the script classes or imported the Arabic fonts. Fix: `tools/play.ps1` and `tools/play.bat` run a headless import first, then the game (commit `5dc5193`).
2. **The beach gave him nothing to do.** He could fly the kite, walk and crawl, "that's it, can't get to any other thing". Nothing told him Sami was off to the right. Fix: Sami calls out when the day begins and the hint names the direction (commit `e016f87`).
3. **The camera never moved and Layla walked off the edge of the screen; the sky was a flat yellow slab.** Cause: `game/scenes/main.tscn` still held three placeholder nodes from the very first session: a `Camera2D` fixed at the screen centre and two full-screen `ColorRect`s (a yellow "sky" and a blue "sea"). That camera entered the tree before any beat, stayed current for the whole game, so no beat camera ever followed the player; the rectangles covered each beat's real sky, skyline and sea. Every bot and every review render loaded beat scenes directly and never went through `main.tscn`, so nothing had ever seen the real start. Fix: `main.tscn` keeps only the end title; every beat camera calls `make_current()`; `tests/shot_main.tscn` renders the real start while walking; `test_core` fails if `main.tscn` ever carries a camera or backdrop again (commit `c7f6c1d`).
4. **The street worked.** His screenshot shows the facades, the water truck and Layla carrying the jerrycan, rendering as intended on his GPU.
5. **"How to reach stairs."** In the home he could not get onto the stairs. Cause: the two flights had their feet against the side walls, under the slabs above, so in a side view no flight could be reached without a jump; the home bot teleported Layla to the roof and never walked the route. Fix: the house is a zigzag now (the ground flight rises to the right, the first-floor flight rises left to the roof), each slab has a one-way stairwell, and the home bot walks the whole route with real physics (commit `61714b3`).
6. **The verdict**, after three stages, in his words (lightly punctuated):

> okay i see the vision. if my dream is v2.0, this is v0.2, very very far back it is.
> what if we generate textures and sounds and stuff using ai tho? ... cuz right now i really wish the assets were better.
> i had silksong and kite runner and life is strange in mind, and this is 0.1% of that. understandable, this is very early stage of development. we fix things layer by layer. but im lacking good development experience, i dont know how to navigate from here. so think like a world class best seller game dev company or even indie dev, and arrange my thoughts into an executable pipeline.
> just the things i notice in the brief 3 stage gameplay i played: the characters look dead, wooden, scarf is weird, eyes are weird, jump and walk animations are weird, the story feels dead, the gameplay feels dry, cuts seem sudden, unfinished gameplay, sounds feel weird.
> so before moving forward, we might wanna nail down the final story, and the final asset course and finalise every single thing that cant be changed down the line once real production starts.

Then, on 30 September: "yes, document everything first, since i might have to shift agents from here".

## What this test proved

- The systems chain and run on the founder's own laptop at full resolution on Forward+ (Vulkan, Iris Xe). That laptop is the minimum-spec machine from now on.
- The founder can run builds, send screenshots and describe what he sees. Playtests with him work; strangers are the next step.
- The world rendering (sky, sea, facades, the home cross-section) reads on his screen as it does in our renders.
- The rest of the gap is craft, not systems: animation, writing, sound, level readability and transitions.

## Lessons for whoever builds next

1. Always verify through the real entry point (`main.tscn`, or `tests/shot_main.tscn` headless under Xvfb) at 1920 by 1010, not by loading a beat scene directly.
2. A bot that teleports proves nothing about a route. Walk it.
3. Try every build from a fresh clone (delete `game/.godot/`, run `tools/play.ps1` or the import command) before sending it.
4. Until the game teaches itself, send the founder a short route guide with each build.
5. The founder reads the first minute harder than anything else. Put the best minute first.
