# Artist brief

For the founder to send (edited as he likes) to an artist who might join SUMUD. The public call to post is in `docs/outreach.md`. Terms (fee, revenue share, credit) are the founder's to set; this brief says what the work is.

## The game in one paragraph

SUMUD is a short 2D narrative game about one family on one street in Gaza City over ten days, from a kite festival to a wedding held in a school courtyard. A twelve-year-old girl, Layla, flies kites. There is no combat. The look is silhouettes lit by scarce warm light, like Limbo and Inside, with colour carried by the sky, the sea and a few objects: kites, bread, fire, the red of tatreez. The score is human voices only. The game is made with Palestinian readers whose notes are binding, and nothing in it is AI-generated.

## Why we need you

The world (skies, sea, buildings, light) is built by code and already looks right. The people do not: they are drawn by code and move like puppets. We need a human hand for the people and for the objects the story touches.

## The work, in order

1. **Character model sheets** for Layla, Sami, Teta, Baba, Mama and Karim, then the neighbours: the silhouette at game size and large, front and side; costume that is modest and true to Gaza (readers review); one readable trait each (Teta's cane and long thobe, Baba's cap and shoulders, Mama's bag of books, Karim's ball, Layla's headscarf and kite, Sami's reel). Characters are in `docs/story/characters.md`.
2. **Three style frames** (`docs/art/direction.md`): the beach at the count, the roof at maghrib, the dark street.
3. **Animation.** Either cleaning and stylising rotoscoped silhouettes filmed by the team, or drawing cut-out characters for a Skeleton2D rig; the animation test decides which (`docs/art/direction.md`). Day 1's list comes first.
4. **Hero props** as silhouettes: the key, kites (including Sami's rebuilt kite), the jerrycan, the paddle boat, the oven, the wedding dress, the school gate, the kiosk.

## Rules

- Your own hand. No AI tools anywhere in the process, including for reference or ideas.
- No women's dancing, no romance, women covered outside the home; the readers and the founder review every character.
- Reference from your own photographs, public-domain or CC0 material. Never trace a photographer's image or footage of real Gazans.
- Delivery as PNG with transparency (and your source files), at twice game size, named as `docs/art/direction.md` specifies.

## What you get

Credit on the store page and in the credits "above the fold" with the Palestinian contributors (the design document's "made with, not for" rule), a copy of the game, and the terms the founder offers.

## First step

A small paid or agreed test: Layla's model sheet and one style frame. If it works for both sides, the rest follows in weekly batches reviewed in the game, not in isolation.
