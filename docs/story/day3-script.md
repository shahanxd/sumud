# Day 3: The dark. Script for the slice

Status: **first draft, 1 October 2026**, written from `docs/story/beat-sheet.md` Day 3 and the design document (first strike, bakery puzzle, title card). English guidance for the Palestinian writer; the readers vet every line. Same conventions as `day1-script.md`: keys `d3.<scene>.<speaker>.<nn>` in `game/data/lines.csv`; Teta's proverbs are placeholders marked [proverb] until the readers supply real ones; nothing may contradict `docs/research/13` to `18`.

**Shape.** An ordinary morning, dread rising all day, the bang, silence, then the warmest night in the game. Two intensity beats only: the run downstairs and the cupped flame. No death, no fail state; a missed run is a white-out and the roof door.

**Spaces.** The two roofs and the house (fajr, the dress, maghrib, under the stairs, night), the street at night (the silent minaret, the bakery, the doors).

## Scene 5: The silent minaret (the street, isha)

**Staging.** The street black. The mosque's speakers are gone with the power. Silence, and then from a roof by the steps to the sea one unaided voice calls the isha adhan: Abu Khalil, 70s, a kuffiyeh round his neck. Until a recording exists (a human muezzin or the friend, with permission) the adhan is a caption and silence, never a synthetic voice.

**Play.** Listen; Layla climbs to see him (crates and a ladder by his room).

## Scene 6: Bread (the bakery, isha)

**Play.** Three characters, switching (Tab): Layla crawls into the rubble for two flour sacks; Baba lifts the beam off the oven door; Teta walks to Abu Ahmad and persuades him (talk). Sami holds the candle: the only light in the room. Then Abu Ahmad cranks by candlelight and Sami counts the turns. Card: Sahih al-Bukhari 2486, on the oven mouth.

## Build notes

**Spaces.** `home.tscn` with the neighbour roof (as `d1_home_asr`), `night_street.tscn` with the bakery interior and Abu Khalil's roof added. **Verbs.** Kite (0), sit and hold (1), the timed run (2), the two-character beam (4), climb (5), three-character switching with crawl, lift, talk (6), candle traversal and cupping (7), sit (8). **Sound.** The hum from fajr (`drone_hum_loop`, faint at fajr, louder by asr), the rumble under the telegraph, the bang alone, the ringing, then only wind and the oven; no synthetic voice for the adhan. **Palette.** `morning` with the Day 3 saturation, `noon`, `dusk`, then `siege_night` from the bang. **Gate.** A stranger gets down the stairs on the first or second try, lights at least two windows, and names the bread or the voice from the roof.
